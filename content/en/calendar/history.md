# Calendar

:::release[Altrone 4 Decent Dragon]{icon="/logos/altrone-4.png" logo="Calendar" name="Calendar" open="true"}
**4.0**

- New design: date highlighting, a header, month navigation.
- Three selection modes: a single day, several days and a range (`mode`, `value`, `onSelect`).
- The language comes from `Application.language`, the first day of the week is derived from the locale.
- Added `showHeader`, `showNavigation`, `showWeekdays`, `showOutsideDays`, `prevIcon` and `nextIcon`.
- Fixed: the weekend color no longer overrides the disabled date style.
- `onMonthChange` and `onHover` receive the native event.
:::
