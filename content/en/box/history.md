**1.3**

- `Box`, `useOffset`, `useMargin` and `usePadding` are deprecated and will be removed in 3.0.

**1.0**

- `Box` is added to the library. It is a helper component for spacing, built on the `useOffset`, `useMargin` and `usePadding` hooks.
:::
