# Notifications

:::release[Altrone 4 Decent Dragon]{icon="/logos/altrone-4.png" logo="Notifications" name="Notifications" open="true"}
**4.0**

- New design and a completely new engine.
- Toasts and notifications share one set of placement settings.
- A notification can have an image, a custom display time and a close condition.
- `useNotifications()` was removed — call the global `showToast`, `showNotification` and `dismissNotification` from anywhere.
- `toastPlacement`, `notificationSide` and `notificationPlacement` were merged into a single corner `notificationPlacement`.
:::
