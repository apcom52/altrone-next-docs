# NavigationList

:::release[Altrone 4 Decent Dragon]{icon="/logos/altrone-4.png" logo="NavigationList" name="NavigationList" open="true"}
**4.0**

- New design.
- Added a fixed header and footer.
- The controls of the items are easy to replace.
- `GroupAction` and `LinkAction` were removed — use the `actions` prop of `Group` and `Link`, which takes an ordinary button.
- `Item` was renamed to `Link`.
- Fixed long labels and titles overflowing the sidebar.
:::
