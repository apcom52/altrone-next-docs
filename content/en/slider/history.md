# Slider

:::release[Altrone 4 Decent Dragon]{icon="/logos/altrone-4.png" logo="Slider" name="Slider" open="true"}
**4.0**

- Renamed from `Range` to `Slider`.
- Improved typing and callbacks: `onValueCommit` renamed to `onChangeEnd`, `direction` to `orientation`, `activeTrackClassName` to `activeClassName`.
- The `fill` variant is always pill-shaped.
- Fixed the position of the current value bubble.
- The page no longer scrolls along with the slider on touch devices.
- Releasing the slider over another element no longer triggers its click.
- Reads `size`, `disabled` and `name` from `Form`.
:::
